ParnHealthSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. About the app

ParnHealthSync is a personal tool used only by its developer and owner. It connects to the owner's own Google account, with the owner's consent, to read the owner's own Fitbit health data through the Google Health API.

2. Data accessed

The app requests read-only access to the owner's Fitbit/Google Health data, such as daily activity, steps, heart rate and sleep, and basic account identification (email address) needed to sign in.

3. How data is used

Data is downloaded to the owner's own computer and saved as files in the owner's private note vault for personal tracking and analysis. It is used for no other purpose.

4. Sharing and storage

The data is not sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising. It is stored only on the owner's own devices and the owner's personal cloud storage. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

ParnHealthSync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's devices until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
